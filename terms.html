What this build is for

Showing an Alexa+ add-on that plans meals, tracks a pantry, and walks a cooking session, built for the Build, Ship, Shape 2026 hackathon. Use it to evaluate the demo, not to run your actual kitchen on.

The shelf-life and nutrition figures are estimates, not food-safety advice

Shelf-life and nutrition numbers come from curated reference tables, labelled as estimates everywhere they're shown. They have not all been independently verified — see BLOCKED.md §H for exactly which rows have and haven't. Do not rely on this build to decide whether food is safe to eat. Use your own judgment, or a source meant to carry that responsibility.

The checkout is a demonstration

Nothing bought through the demo store is real. No order ships, no card is charged, and the payment instruments it offers are fictional. The receipt it produces is for demonstrating the flow, not a record of an actual purchase.

No account linking yet

Account-bound features run behind a single shared demonstration account, not your own verified identity. Do not use this build for anything you'd consider private.

Availability and changes
